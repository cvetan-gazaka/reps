Reps - iPhone app (installable web app)

1. Put this whole folder on any HTTPS static host (free options):
   - Netlify Drop: drag the folder onto https://app.netlify.com/drop
   - GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages or Vercel
2. On your iPhone, open the site's address in SAFARI (not Chrome).
3. Tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen", then Add.
4. Open Reps from your home screen: full screen, own icon, works offline.

Notes:
- Your workouts are stored on the phone. The home-screen app has its own
  storage, so start logging in the home-screen app, not in Safari.
- Deleting the app from the home screen deletes its data.
